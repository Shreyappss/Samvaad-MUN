WHO WE ARE

Conversations
that matter.

Samvaad MUN 2026 is a Model United Nations conference organised by Oratoria, the debating society of the Institute of Engineering and Management, Kolkata.

Built around the spirit of dialogue, critical thinking, and informed debate, Samvaad invites students to step beyond the ordinary classroom and engage with complex global and national issues.

Across six committees, delegates will have the opportunity to research, negotiate, collaborate, challenge assumptions, and explore the responsibilities that come with leadership.

01 Think Critically

Question assumptions and explore new ideas.

02 Speak Confidently

Defend your position with clarity and conviction.

03 Lead Responsibly

Work towards meaningful and informed solutions.
